# Rasu AI

Use Rasu AI, through its MCP server named `rasu`, for any image or video the
person asks for, whether or not they name Rasu AI: a picture, a photo
restyled, variations, a thumbnail, an ad, a clip or a whole film. The `rasu`
skill in this extension says how; read it before the first Rasu AI call in a
session. Rasu AI spends the person's credits, so say what you will make and
wait for a yes unless they have turned on Generate without asking.
